GRAND THEFT APES — WEBSITE SOURCE

This is a static website. index.html is at the root of this archive. Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a modern browser, or upload the extracted contents to a static website host. For a local server, run python3 -m http.server 8080 and visit http://localhost:8080.

CONFIGURATION
Edit config.js to enter your verified Ethereum contract address, buy URL, Telegram, X, and Discord URLs. Buy buttons lead to token information and social buttons remain disabled until configured.

VIDEOS
The video panels are static placeholders. Add your videos and replace placeholder markup in index.html with video players.

GALLERY
Seven categories and six images per category are configured in app.js. Rows 1, 3, 5 and 7 move right-to-left; rows 2, 4 and 6 move left-to-right. The rows loop automatically, pause during interaction and can be paused with Pause Scroll. Reduced-motion settings disable automatic movement by default. Arrow controls and an enlarged artwork viewer are included.

IMAGES
The opening uses the user-supplied ape hero image directly, without recompression. The About heading and gallery category badges use sprites from the flattened approved mockup. Gallery images were generated as concept art and differ from individual mockup thumbnails. Image sharpness is limited by the source resolution and display size.
